# Dataset Card: Multilingual VLM Benchmark

## Purpose & Motivation
To provide a rigorous, culturally diverse, and multilingual evaluation framework for Vision-Language Models (VLMs), moving beyond English-centric scene text datasets.

## Composition
- **Total Size**: Target 1,000 images (v1.0.0).
- **Domains**: Scene Text, Documents, Charts, Receipts, Transportation.
- **Languages/Scripts**: Global coverage across 20+ languages and 10+ scripts.

## Collection Process
Images are sourced from open-data repositories (Wikimedia Commons, CC-BY datasets) and physical photography. All data undergoes strict PII blurring and double-blind annotation.

## Licensing & Ethics
- **License**: The benchmark annotations and framework are MIT licensed. The underlying images retain their original CC-BY/CC0/Public Domain licenses.
- **Ethics**: Strict exclusion of PII, children, and medical data.

## Limitations
- Predominantly urban environments due to the nature of dense scene text.
- Synthetic document inclusion where real documents are legally prohibited due to PII.

## Maintenance & Future Releases
- Actively maintained with periodic audits.
