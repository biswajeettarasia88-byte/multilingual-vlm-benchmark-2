# Annotation Governance

## Qualifications
- Annotators must pass an initial schema qualification test.
- For complex scripts (e.g., Arabic, Han), native or highly proficient speakers are required.

## Quality Assurance & Audits
- **Double Annotation**: 100% of the dataset requires double-blind annotation.
- **Inter-Annotator Agreement (IAA)**: A minimum threshold of 95% spatial overlap (IoU) and 99% text transcription match is required.
- **Conflict Resolution**: Disagreements are escalated to a designated Senior Reviewer.
- **Periodic Audits**: 5% of the accepted dataset will undergo periodic randomized re-audits.
