# Benchmark Collection Campaign

## Targets
- **100 images**: Initial proof of concept, focused on high-density urban environments.
- **500 images**: Expansion to complex documents (receipts, charts, passports).
- **1000 images**: Final benchmark-v1 size, highly distributed across 20+ countries.

## Target Geography & Languages
Target countries include India, Japan, UAE, South Korea, Germany, China, France, Russia, UK, Kenya, Thailand, Morocco, Singapore, Switzerland, Egypt, Brazil, Italy, Israel, Nepal.
Target languages cover English, Hindi, Japanese, Arabic, Korean, German, Chinese, French, Russian, Swahili, Thai, Tamil, Italian, Portuguese, Hebrew, Nepali, Gujarati.

## Annotation Workflow & Milestones
- **Weekly Milestones**: Batch processing of 100 images per week.
- **Data Collection**: Photographers and open source repositories.
